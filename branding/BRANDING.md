# Qix — brand guide

**Proposed display name:** Qix
**Tagline:** *The 1981 line game, reborn in the browser.*

## Why this name

You don't rename Qix — the game is the brand. The mark just gives the repo the game's own protagonist.

## The mark

The Qix itself — the tumbling bundle of colored line segments — above the player's claimed edge and marker.
